Safety Guidelines

Your safety is important. Please follow these guidelines when using PartnerConnect.

1. Protect Personal Information

Never share your password, OTP, bank details, payment information or other sensitive information with another person.

2. Take Your Time

Get to know people gradually. Be cautious if someone pressures you to share information, send money or make quick decisions.

3. Watch for Scams

Be careful of anyone asking for money, gift cards, financial assistance or urgent payments. Do not send money to someone you have only met online.

4. Meeting Someone

If you decide to meet someone you connected with online, choose a safe public location and tell a trusted person about your plans.

5. Report Suspicious Behaviour

If you notice harassment, impersonation, fraud, threats or other suspicious behaviour, stop communicating with the person and report the issue through the contact method provided by PartnerConnect.

6. Trust Your Instincts

If something feels unsafe or uncomfortable, end the interaction. Your personal safety should always come first.

7. Emergency Situations

If you believe you are in immediate danger, contact your local emergency services or a trusted person.
